# Myanmar Earthquake Disaster Assessment Data Repository

## Project Overview

This repository stores field survey data from the 2025 M7.9 Myanmar Earthquake disaster assessment, including on-site investigation photos and data analysis files.

## Data Structure

```
├── picture/          # Field survey photos (organized by date/location)
├── Bridge Information of Myanmar Seismic Stations.xlsx
├── Rail Damage & Road Coordinates.xlsx
├── SFFM_SPECTRA_SA.xlsx
├── highway bridge SFM.xlsx
└── railway bridges SFM.xlsx
```

## File Description

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Bridge Information of Myanmar Seismic Stations.xlsx` | Bridge information of seismic stations in Myanmar |
| `Rail Damage & Road Coordinates.xlsx` | Railway damage and road coordinates |
| `SFFM_SPECTRA_SA.xlsx` | SFFM spectrum analysis data |
| `highway bridge SFM.xlsx` | Highway bridge SFM analysis |
| `railway bridges SFM.xlsx` | Railway bridge SFM analysis |

## Photo Directories

- `20250418（地震局Lewe东）/` - Survey photos of Seismic Bureau (East Lewe)
- `20250419（Lewe东西5度界）/` - Survey photos of Lewe East-West boundary
- `20250420（Tatkon-Gwegyi）/` - Survey photos of Tatkon-Gwegyi section
- `20250421（内业）/` - Office documentation photos
- `20250422（内比都至曼德勒）/` - Survey photos of Naypyidaw-Mandalay highway
- `20250423（Kyar Ni kan 及曼德勒水处理厂）/` - Survey photos of water treatment plant
- `20250425（市供电局）/` - Survey photos of City Power Supply Bureau
- `20250427（曼德勒至仰光高速）/` - Survey photos of Mandalay-Yangon Expressway
- `City_Hall_AVA_Bridge/` - Survey photos of City Hall AVA Bridge
- `Substation_Traffic_Construction_Bureau/` - Survey photos of Substation & Traffic Construction Bureau
- `Yangon-Naypyidaw_Expressway/` - Survey photos of Yangon-Naypyidaw Expressway

## Data Source

Field survey data from China-Myanmar Earthquake Disaster Assessment Team (April 2025)
